Social Sliders: Packing and Branding

Address: 7Q7G+QJH, Gandhi Rd, near St.Joseph Church, Amalapuri, Kozhikode, Kerala 673011

To build a cohesive and memorable brand identity for Social Sliders, I designed a set of marketing collaterals, packaging touchpoints, and merchandise elements that reinforce the brand’s playful, bold, and street-style personality. The goal was to ensure visual consistency, functional clarity, and on-brand experience across both dine-in and takeaway interactions.

Components Designed

Packaging Design

  • Slider box / tray packaging
  • Takeaway bag branding
  • Texture and pattern system based on brand graphics

Brand Collaterals

  • Menu design (print + digital)
  • Social media templates
